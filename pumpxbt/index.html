PumpXBT

The trading desk that runs itself, in public.

It watches every launch on pump.fun and scores callers on their record, not their reach. Creator fees are claimed on a fixed cycle. Part of each claim buys back and burns, and the rest funds the agent. You can see every number, and when a feed is down the number shows as a dash.

02 The loop

Fees in. Burn out.

Five steps. Each one shows who runs it today and its live figure, so you can see which parts are automated and which aren't yet.

    Route per claim is set in pumpxbt-bot/flywheel/memcoin.config.json. It runs on the shared memcoinz flywheel worker.

    03 Intelligence

    It reads the chain, not the timeline.

    It doesn't trade on vibes. It runs a signal stack over live pump.fun state, and it sits out when the tape isn't worth trading.

    agent callouts feed offline
    whentokencallscorestatus

    No callouts on record yet. They show up here as soon as the agent's ledger API is connected and it queues one.

    04 Supply

    Performance is deflationary.

    The buyback share of every fee claim buys PUMPXBT on the open market and sends it to the incinerator. Burned supply isn't a number we report. It's read from the mint itself: one billion minus what exists now.
