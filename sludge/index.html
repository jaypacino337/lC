HOW THE SCORE WORKS
  • VOLUME 0–35 — log scale: $1K/24h scores 0, $10M/24h scores 35.
  • LIQUIDITY 0–25 — log scale on the deepest DEX pool: ~$3K is 0, ~$3M is 25.
  • TURNOVER 0–15 — 24h volume ÷ liquidity. 0.3×–8× is healthy; it decays to 0 by 80×. Over 30× is flagged as wash-trade risk.
  • AGE 0–15 — log scale up to 30 days. Surviving is a signal; under an hour is flagged fresh.
  • FLOW 0–10 — share of 24h transactions that are sells. 35–65% is a two-sided market; under 5% means almost nobody sells — check whether they can.
  • INFLATION −8 / −20 — market cap over 30× liquidity is thin; over 60× is inflated: the price is thin air.
  • VERDICT — 65+ KEEPS, 40–64 SIMMERING, under 40 DISSOLVES. Any red flag caps it at SIMMERING.
