03 / Launch Office

Build under HOLDCO.

Builders can apply to launch independent companies through the HOLDCO ecosystem.

Approved subsidiaries receive access to shared infrastructure designed to reduce the time between idea and working product.

What subsidiaries get

    What HOLDCO gets

    Approved subsidiaries may route a clearly disclosed portion of creator or product revenue into the HOLDCO ecosystem.

    Every allocation must be visible and documented — published in the group's financials with onchain proof.

    Applications are reviewed based on concept quality, feasibility and ability to ship.
