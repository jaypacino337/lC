# HamGPT ($HAMGPT): launch kit

Rebrand of LocalLM / FreeLM ($LLM). Same idea (free-tier model routing, an AI workspace and one credit balance for chat and API), now built around a character: **Ham**, a hamster in a headset who routes your prompts through free tiers and hoards every free token in his cheeks.

**Description** (pump.fun allows about 250 characters):

> The AI that never pays for tokens. Ham is a hamster in a headset who routes your prompts through free-tier AI models and stuffs his cheeks with every free token he finds. One balance for chat and API.

## After launch

1. In `js/app.js`, fill in `CONFIG.ca`, `x`, `telegram`, `pump`, `dex` (and `app` once a real workspace exists). The CA buttons and social pills switch on by themselves.
2. In `index.html`, change `og:image` and `twitter:image` to absolute URLs (`https://yourdomain/assets/og.png`).
3. The chat on the page is a scripted demo. If you later connect the real FreeLM router, replace `ask()` in `js/app.js` and remove the "demo" labels. Until then, leave the labels on.
